## How long your answers should be

There is **no long-answer section**.

| Section | What to write |
|---|---|
| MCQ Level I and II | the letter only |
| Short answer Level I | a word or formula, e.g. `O(n)` |
| Short answer Level II | only the final result, in the format asked, e.g. `2(B) − 4(R) − …` or `c = 3, n0 = 10`. Do the working on the draft paper |
